# Tree textures

- `scan_red_oak_*` and `../red_oak.oltree`: cut down by
  `tools/trees/scan_tree.py red_oak --atlas` from "Realistic HD Northern red
  oak (35/138)" by PlantCatalog
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-hd-northern-red-oak-35138-c7c08439d3d34b9385bf2d2237c34ad2),
  licensed CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed:
  decimated bark, leaves regrouped into cluster cards, leaf-spray atlas
  rendered from the model's own leaves, textures resized.

The other scanned trees' textures are CC0 from Poly Haven and are fetched by
`tools/fetch_free_assets.py`, not committed.
